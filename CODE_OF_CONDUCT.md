# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors regardless of background or experience. Discuss code and evidence respectfully; assume that people may have different constraints and levels of familiarity.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sharing private information without permission, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private conversations or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and based on behavior. For concerns, contact the maintainer through their available GitHub profile channels; use GitHub's **Report abuse** flow when a private contact is unavailable. Do not reveal private incident details in a public issue.
